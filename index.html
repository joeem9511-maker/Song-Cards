Paste a Spotify track link for each card (Spotify: Share → Copy link). Once connected, title, artist and year fill in automatically. The year is the album’s release year, so fix it by hand for remasters and compilations.

  1. Open developer.spotify.com/dashboard, log in and tap Create app. Tick Web API.
  2. Add this as a Redirect URI and save:
  3. Copy the app’s Client ID and paste it here:
  4. Tap Connect and approve access. You need Spotify Premium, and the Spotify app must be installed on this phone.

Playing: open the Spotify app once and start any song so your phone shows up as a device. After that, scanning a card starts the song in the background. Don’t open Spotify during the game, since it shows the title.

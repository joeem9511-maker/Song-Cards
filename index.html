Song Cards

Scan a card, hear the song, place it on your timeline.

?

Paste a Spotify track link for each card (Spotify: Share → Copy link). Once connected, title, artist and year fill in automatically. The year is the album’s release year, so fix it by hand for remasters and compilations.

Spotify only lets this app read playlists you own or collaborate on.
A4 sheets fill each page edge to edge with QR cards, each with its song card next to it. Card size is a minimum: the app enlarges the cards a little so they fill the page width exactly, stay perfectly square, and sit centered vertically. Most home printers can’t print the last 4–5 mm, so set Margin to 5 if edges get cut off.

Playing: open the Spotify app once and start any song so your phone shows up as a device. After that, scanning a card starts the song in the background. Don’t open Spotify during the game, since it shows the title.
